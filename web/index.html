Data

Active-fire detections from NASA FIRMS (MODIS and VIIRS), part of NASA Earth Science Data and Information System. Weekly counts per grid cell; MODIS is converted to the VIIRS scale using the years both sensors operated.

Method

Methods borrowed from industrial alarm management (seasonal baseline, hysteresis, quality codes). PyroHistorian is an analysis and decision-support view, not a control system.

Transparency

Built with AI assistance (Claude, Anthropic), reviewed and tested by the team. Created for the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026; not affiliated with or endorsed by NASA.
